# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community. Be respectful, assume good intent,
focus criticism on ideas and code, protect privacy, and never post credentials or personal data.

Unacceptable conduct includes harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, sexualized attention,
spam, impersonation, or attempts to bypass HumanLeg safety controls. Maintainers may edit, remove or
reject contributions and restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to `community@humanleg.ai`.
